Department
Version 0.1 (draft) · Scope: roles, features, workflows, approvals, permissions, integrations
1. Purpose and Scope
The Academics department is fully focused on education. It covers teaching, learning,
assessment, student records and academic administration. It also raises requests to, and
receives requests from, every other department (store, finance, HR, transport, IT,
housekeeping, etc.).
Users in this department: Director of Academics (DA), Principal, Coordinator, Teacher,
Student, PRM (Front Office), Librarian.
Cross-department roles that interact with Academics: MD (views everything), HR,
Finance, Store, Transport Manager, Security, IT Support.
2. Roles and Hierarchy
2.1 Hierarchy (approval authority, high to low)
MD → Director of Academics (DA) → Principal → Coordinator → Teacher → Student
PRM (Front Office) and Librarian are service roles that sit alongside the academic
hierarchy and report into it.
2.2 Role responsibilities
Role Primary responsibility

4. Module Details and Workflows
4.1 Dashboard and Analytics
Every user has a role-based dashboard with graphs and charts.
Clicking any chart opens the detailed underlying data.
Examples: Student – marks trend, attendance %; Teacher – class performance, pending
approvals; Coordinator – multi-class comparison; Principal / DA – school-wide
academics; MD – all departments, comparisons and audit data.
4.2 User Information System (UIS)
A lifetime record for every user. A student's record runs from Grade 1 to Grade 10 (and
beyond) without breaks.
Student record contains:
Basic information, parent details, sibling information (sibling in same school is
displayed)
Uploaded documents, certificates, achievements
Academic history: marks, exams, activities, extra classes
Attendance history
Fee details: paid / pending, concessions (sibling, staff-child, special cases)
Transport: school bus (with vehicle details) or own transport
Complaints, disciplinary and other activity history
Staff record contains: basic info, documents, activity history, achievements.
Who updates:
Student data → Class Teacher (class mentor)
Staff data → HR and the respective Department Head
Who can view:
Own profile → the user
Student profile → their teachers and the inter-department chain (Teacher, Coordinator,
Principal, DA, MD, PRM)

4.6 Exams, Question Bank, Marks and Results

to the ERP (device server: iClock).
Synced to the respective Teacher and higher ranks.

No punch = automatically Absent

If a leave request exists and is approved, the status auto-updates to Leave

8. Confirmed Decisions
Updates from review. These supersede the earlier draft wording in sections 4 and 6 where
they differ.
1 . Star rating categories: Student categories and staff categories are different. Both sets
are dynamic and configured by the DA (add, rename or disable categories).
Categories are not fixed in the system.
2. Marks approval: Same chain as lesson plan: Teacher → Coordinator → Principal → DA,
then published.
3. Complaint escalation: Fully automatic. If a complaint is not solved or not replied to
within 24 hours, it escalates to the next higher position. Separately, if it was resolved
but the complainant clicks Not Satisfied, it also escalates to the higher position. Full
history is retained.
4. Corrections: No one can change or delete saved data, and there is no correction
workflow. Entry screens must therefore use validation, confirmation and a preview
step before final submit.
5. Parent access: Via the student profile only. No separate parent login.
6. Gate pass approvers: Chosen by the requester (PRM) at request time. The PRM
selects who must approve; the chain is not fixed.
7. Attendance finalisation: Automatic finalisation after a time limit (exact cutoff time to
be set in settings).
8. Evaluation categories: Evaluation is by examination type (e.g., Midterm, CA, RCT).
Each type is a category and types are configurable.
9. Extra-curricular activities: Badminton, dance, karate, rifle shooting and more. Each
activity has its own separate coach. The activity list and coaches are managed by
PRM.
1 0. Library rules: 1 book per student, 5 books per staff. A user who wants to buy a book
submits a request from the portal and it is reviewed. The library also maintains a
missing books list and a book inventory.

9. Additional Decisions and Recommendations
Confirmed
Attendance timings: Student and staff timings are different, and each department has
its own timings. Cutoff times for automatic Present/Absent finalisation are
configurable per user group and per department in settings (no single fixed time).
Permission request: A separate request type for short absence, limited to a maximum
of 2 hours per request. Approved by the Department Head (and HR for staff). On
approval, attendance shows as Permission rather than Absent or Leave.
Library duration: Borrow duration and fine rules are custom-configurable (by user
type) in library settings. Fixed limits remain 1 book per student and 5 per staff.
Recommendation: exam weightage
Make weightage configurable by the DA per class and per exam type, with the final result
computed automatically. Suggested pattern, editable at any time before an exam cycle
starts:
Why: weightage is a school policy that changes by grade (for example, lower grades lean
more on continuous assessment), so it should be a setting rather than fixed in code. Once
an exam cycle starts, the weightage is locked, which fits the no-edit rule.
Next department to document (suggested order): Non-Academics (Joint Director, Stores,
Transport, Housekeeping/Maintenance, IT, Camera Room, File Room) → HR → Finance →
MD → Admin → Audit.
