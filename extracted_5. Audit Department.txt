1. Purpose and Scope
The Audit department runs process audits and quality audits across the school to ensure
academic excellence, administrative efficiency, compliance and continuous improvement.
In the ERP, the Audit department turns the SOP's daily, weekly, monthly, fortnightly and
quarterly audit plan into scheduled audit tasks, checklists, findings, corrective actions
(CAPA) and reports, replacing the Google Sheets and registers used today.
Scope (from the SOP):
Academics: teaching-learning, assessments, lesson planning, student performance.
Administration: IT, Maintenance, Housekeeping, Stores, HR, Finance, Transport,
Security.
Student and parent services: counselling, communication, co-curricular activities.
Objectives: compliance with school policies, board norms and statutory requirements;
monitor process effectiveness; identify gaps and risks; recommend corrective and
preventive actions; drive continuous improvement.

4.3 Quality Audits
Quality audits are carried out by SPQA and PQA members on academic delivery.
Components from the SOP include: class observation, notebook audit (5 notebooks from
2 classes), journal audit, auxiliary activity audit, homework audit, documentation
(DSR/GSS), Grade 1 to 8 Zero Hour, KG2 to Grade 3 reading program, spell-well audit,
observee meetings, DEM, roll call, assembly, ASA observation, weekly worksheets,
bulletin boards, CA score sheet update, KPI audit, BFIT and academic lesson plan audits,
CA question paper audit, event risk management, smart tail evaluation, answer script
evaluation, exam audit, calendar activities audit and review meeting, additional audit
(circulars and monthly report), and lab audit.
Each item carries a sampling rule and frequency (for example days x classes x audit
members) and a duration, as in the SOP.
4.4 Audit Checklists and Process Checks
Each audit component has a checklist built on the SOP's five process-check
components:
1 . Input verification: are resources, data or materials correct before starting?
2. Procedure adherence: are team members following the correct steps?
3. Output review: is the result consistent and timely?
4. Documentation: is evidence recorded and traceable?
5. Feedback and correction: are issues identified and corrected quickly?
Checklist items are maintained by JD Audit. Executives record the result, remarks and
evidence (photo/file).
4.5 Findings, Ratings and Risk Classification
Each finding is rated Compliant, Minor Non-Conformity or Major Non-Conformity.
Each risk is classified High, Medium or Low.
Findings are shared with the management and the respective department heads.

4.6 Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA)
For each non-conformity, JD Audit assigns a corrective action with an owner and
deadline.
The owner records the action taken; the auditor verifies closure.
Overdue actions escalate to the next higher authority and to the MD [Proposed].
Closed items go into the continuous improvement log.
4.7 Follow-Up and Action Taken Reports (ATR)
Track implementation of corrective measures.
Follow-up on ATRs for the QAC-BOG, Strategic Plan and internal action plans.
Verification of closure of non-conformities before an item is marked closed.
4.8 KPI / BSC Scoring
The SOP refers to the Audit BSC-KRA-KPI enclosure (14.05.25) for KPIs.
Audit results feed the department and staff BSC audit score, which HR uses in
performance review, increment and promotion.
Detailed KPIs to be added once the enclosure is shared.
4.9 System Audit Trail
Part of the Audit role in the ERP, visible to the MD and JD Audit:
Login details (who, when, from where).
Entry details (who created which record and when).
Page downloads, print and download logs.
Because no user can edit or delete data, the trail is complete and permanent.
4.10 Reports, Dashboard and Continuous Improvement
Quarterly review meetings with audit findings (SOP section 10).
Audit results integrated into the school improvement plan; benchmarking with peer
institutions.
Dashboard for the MD and JD Audit: audits due and completed, findings by
department, major non-conformities, risk levels, CAPA status, trend over time,
department comparisons.
Filters and PDF/Excel export on all pages.
