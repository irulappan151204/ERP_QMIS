Version 0.1 (draft, built from your notes and the rules already set in Academics, Non-
Academics and HR; items marked [Proposed] are my suggestions for you to confirm)
1. Purpose and Scope
Finance manages all money in the school: student fee collection, concessions, vendor
and payroll payments, budgets, approval limits and full accounting. It covers Academics,
Non-Academics, Transport and all other departments. Every financial detail is visible to
the MD, together with an audit trail.
2. Roles
MD → Finance Head [Proposed] → Accountant → Fee Collection Staff / Cashier
Role names can be adjusted. The ERP follows the same rule as other departments: no edit
and no delete after saving.
3. Module Map
Role Responsibility
Finance Head Sets budgets and approval limits, approves
vouchers and statements, oversees all accounts.
Accountant Maintains books and ledgers, vouchers, bank
reconciliation, financial statements.
Fee Collection Staff (Cashier) Collects fees, issues receipts, handles counter and
POS payments.
MD Full visibility of all finance data and audit logs; final
approvals.

4. Module Details and Workflows
4.1 Fee Structure and Configuration
Fee heads defined by Finance: tuition, admission, transport, books, activities, events,
others.
Fee structure by class and academic year, with installment schedule (monthly, term-
wise, yearly).
Due dates and late-fee rules are settings [Proposed].
Fee data per student is shown in the student profile (paid or pending).
4.2 Fee Collection and Receipts
Payment through counter, online and POS (see 4.7). Receipt generated automatically.

4.3 Fee Reminders and WhatsApp Billing
Monthly fee reminders are sent automatically before and after the due date.
When a payment is made, the bill/receipt is sent to WhatsApp automatically, and is
also available in the student profile.
Reminder timing is configurable [Proposed].
4.4 Concessions
Concessions are approved by users with authority (limits set by Finance) and are
visible in the student profile and to the MD.
No edit/delete: a change is a new entry with date and approver.
4.5 Yearly Fee Increase
Finance can apply the yearly increase by percentage or by amount.
Applied by class (different increases for different classes).
Takes effect for the next academic year; the old fee structure stays in history.
4.6 Transport, Activity and Book Fees
Transport fee: based on route/distance; student details and payment status sync to
the Transport Manager screen.

Extra-curricular activity fees (badminton, dance, karate, rifle shooting, etc.): enrolled
by PRM, collected by Finance.
Book fees: configured under book fee heads; library purchase requests come through

4.7 POS / Card / UPI Integration
Finance can sync with POS machines such as Paytm swipe machines.
Payments received on the machine are posted to the ERP fee collection automatically
[Proposed] (subject to the vendor's API).
Payment mode recorded: cash, card, UPI, bank transfer, cheque, online.
4.8 Budget Management
Finance defines monthly, yearly and event budgets by department.
Requests (store purchases, tickets involving cost) check against the budget; over-
budget items are flagged and redirected.
Budget vs actual reports by department and period.
4.9 Approval Limits
Finance sets approval limits for each role (Academics and Non-Academics alike).
A Department Head can approve up to the limit; above the limit the request is
redirected to Finance by the head.
Limits also apply to salary advances and loans (set jointly with HR).
4.10 Payment Vouchers and Requests
Payment voucher for vendor payments, payroll and refunds.
Flow: request/bill → department approval → Finance verification → voucher → payment
→ posted to books.
Purchases follow the Store workflow (vendor comparison by the Store Officer).
4.11 Accounting: Books and Ledgers
Day book, online book, general ledger, cash book, bank book, chart of accounts,
journal vouchers.
Entries post automatically from fee collection, payment vouchers and payroll.
Chart of accounts is configurable; manual journal vouchers require approval.

4.12 Bank Reconciliation
Match bank statement entries to the bank book; list unmatched and pending items.
Reconciliation reports by bank account and period.
4.13 Financial Statements
Income and expenditure, balance sheet, trial balance, cash flow [Proposed].
Period-based, department-wise and fund-wise views (Academics, Non-Academics,
Transport, etc.).
4.14 Payroll Payments (with HR)
HR prepares payroll; Finance verifies and pays through a voucher.
Salary advances, loans and incentives deduct/pay through payroll as defined by HR.
4.15 Reports and Analytics
Fee collection, pending fees, defaulters, collection by class/mode/date, concession
reports, budget vs actual, expense by department, payment status, bank reports.
All pages have filters and PDF/Excel export (with column selection).
4.16 MD Dashboard and Audit
Charts, diagrams and comparisons: collection trend, pending amount, expense by
department, budget use, class-wise performance, year-on-year comparison.
Audit visibility for the MD: login details, entry details, page downloads, print/download
logs.
Detailed audit processes are in the separate Audit document.
4.17 Settings and Configuration
Fee heads, installments, late fee, reminders, concession types, tax/PF rates
[Proposed], payment modes, roles and limits, financial year.

8. Confirmed Decisions
Updates from review. These supersede earlier wording in sections 1 to 7 where they differ.
1 . Finance roles: Two roles: Finance Head and Finance Assistant. Both have the same
responsibilities; the Finance Head is the approval authority. Wherever the draft says
Accountant or Cashier, it means Finance Assistant.
2. Fee heads: Include activity fees such as taekwondo, rifle shooting and the other
extra-curricular activities, in addition to tuition, admission, transport, books and
events.
3. Installments: Configurable (monthly, term-wise, yearly or custom).
4. Late fee: Yes, with custom adjustable rules (fixed or percentage, grace period, and by
class or fee head).
5. Refunds: Refunds apply to vendors and other cases (for example excess payment,
caution deposit, TC), not only to students.
6. POS / gateway: Paytm swipe machine and gateway.
7. WhatsApp: Bills are sent through the WhatsApp Business API.
8. Financial year: April to March.
9. Tax: Tax handling (GST/TDS and related reports) is available as an option that Finance
can switch on when required.

Transport paid/unpaid check, Concession, Staff Salary Consolidation).
