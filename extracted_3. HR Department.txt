1. Purpose and Scope
HR manages the full employee lifecycle: recruitment, onboarding, records, attendance
and leave, training, performance, payroll and exit. HR is the only department that creates
staff users in the ERP. Staff data is maintained by HR and the respective Department
Head.
2. Core Rule: Staff User Creation
Only HR can enter a staff member into the system. HR enters all the data about the user
in one place; nobody else can create a staff user.
Flow:
1 . HR enters the staff profile (basic details, department, role, documents, joining details).
2. On saving, the system creates the user and sends an email with a temporary
password to the staff member's registered email.
3. On first login, the user is forced to change the password.
4. The user then works in the ERP according to the role defined by HR (permissions,
dashboard and modules follow the role).
Department / role switch: HR (and no one else) can switch a staff member's department
or role. The change is saved as a new entry with date and approver; the previous
assignment stays in history.
Rules:
No edit and no delete once saved; changes are made only through defined actions
(transfer, promotion, exit) that keep history.
A staff member can view their own profile only; Department Head and HR can view
staff in scope; MD can view all.
Student users are not created here. They are created automatically when the
admission payment is made (see Academics).

5.4 Interview Scheduling and Feedback
HR schedules interviews and assigns a panel member from another department. For
example, for an academic post an academic staff member is selected to take the
interview.
The panel member gets a notification, takes the interview, and submits feedback and
ratings in the ERP.
HR consolidates ratings for the decision.
5.5 Offer Letter and Onboarding
Flow:
1 . Candidate selected → offer letter generated by HR → approved by HR Head / MD.
2. Candidate accepts → HR enters staff data and the system creates the user (section 2).
3. Onboarding checklist (documents, ID card/RFID card, email, system access, uniform,
orientation) is assigned to the responsible departments.
4. Checklist items are completed and confirmed by each department.
5.6 New Joiner Observation and Shadow Mentor
A shadow mentor is assigned to each new joiner.
Observation period with periodic feedback from the mentor and Department Head.
Ends with a confirmation decision (confirmed, extended, not confirmed).
5.7 Attendance and Leave
Attendance is captured automatically from the RFID device (staff punch daily) and
synced to the ERP.
Leave request: employee requests → approved or denied by HR and the respective
Department Head.
Permission request: short absence up to 2 hours, approved by the Department Head
(and HR). Attendance shows Permission.
Approved leave automatically updates attendance to Leave; no punch and no leave =
Absent.
Emergency leave: the Principal or DA reassigns the teacher's periods (Academics);
other departments' heads reassign duties.
Department-wise and staff-wise timings are configurable (different timings per
department).
Leave balance by type (casual, sick, earned, etc.) [Proposed].

5.8 Training
Training records per employee.
Training schedule (topic, trainer, date, audience).
Training feedback from participants about the session and trainer.
Attendance for training and certificates uploaded to the employee record.
5.9 Performance Review and Comparison
Periodic performance review by Department Head with defined parameters.
Employee self-assessment [Proposed].
Performance comparison across staff in the same department or role, and across
departments for HR Head and MD.
Inputs include star ratings, attendance, training and BSC score.
5.10 BSC Audit Score
Balanced Scorecard score recorded per employee/department as part of performance.
Used in increment and promotion decisions.
Scoring parameters are defined by the audit team (see the Audit document).
5.11 Increment and Promotion
Flow:
1 . Department Head recommends using performance and BSC data.
2. HR Head reviews.
3. MD approves.
4. New salary/designation becomes effective on a defined date; the change is added as a
new record and flows to payroll.
5.12 Payroll and Payslip
Monthly payroll processing from attendance, leave, deductions, allowances, advances,
loans and bonuses.
Payslip generated per employee and visible in their profile.
Payroll is sent to Finance for payment voucher and bank payment.
Payroll lock after approval; corrections only through adjustment entries in the next
cycle.

5.13 Salary Advance and Loan
Employee requests an advance or loan with amount and reason.
Approval: Department Head → HR Head → MD (limits set by Finance).
Repayment schedule and monthly deduction in payroll; balance visible to employee
and HR.
5.14 Employee Bonus
Bonus rules and amounts defined by HR/Finance; approved by MD.
Credited through payroll.
5.15 Memo, Suspension and Disciplinary Action
Memo: issued by Department Head or HR, acknowledged by the employee.
Suspension and disciplinary action: initiated by Department Head or HR, reviewed by
HR Head, approved by MD.
Employee response and decision are recorded; entries are permanent history.
5.16 Employee Child (Fee Concession)
Staff children studying in the school are linked to the staff profile.
Special fee concession for employees' children, shown in the student profile and
applied by Finance.
Other concessions (sibling, special cases) are handled in Finance; the staff-child link is
maintained here.
5.17 Exit Formalities
Flow:
1 . Resignation or termination initiated.
2. Notice period, handover and no-dues clearance from each department (Store, IT,
Library, Finance, Transport, etc.).
3. Exit interview and feedback.
4. Final settlement through payroll/Finance.
5. HR deactivates the user (history retained; no deletion).
6. Relieving/experience letter generated.

3. Leave types and quotas: Defined by HR as settings (types and yearly quotas can be
added and changed for future periods).
4. Payroll components: All of the following are available: PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax and
allowances. HR chooses which ones apply per employee or salary structure.
5. Performance review cycles: Mostly monthly, half-yearly and yearly. The cycle is
chosen when the review is set up.
6. BSC: Defined by HR (parameters and scoring), and the audit team reviews it.
7. Observation period: HR chooses the period for each new joiner.
8. Advance and loan limits: Set jointly by Finance and HR, and they are fixed (not
decided case by case).
9. Staff-child concession: Custom per employee. Some staff receive a high concession
and others a low one. HR records the concession against the employee, and Finance
applies it to the fee.
10. Added Module: Incentives
Incentives are paid through payroll in addition to salary and bonus. Three types:
Proposed handling (please confirm)
Extra work time: measured from the RFID punch beyond the scheduled time, or from
an extra-duty request, approved by the Department Head, then verified by HR and
sent to payroll.
Admission referral: the referrer is tagged on the PRM enquiry or admission record. The
incentive becomes payable only after the admission payment is made.
Staff referral: the referrer is tagged on the candidate record. The bonus becomes
payable after the new joiner is confirmed (end of the observation period).
Incentive amounts or rates are settings defined by HR with Finance, and every
incentive keeps a record: who, why, amount, approver, payroll month.
Incentive How it works
Extra work time incentive Staff who work beyond their scheduled time (extra
time, extra duty) receive an incentive.
Admission referral incentive Staff who refer a student who then gets admitted
receive an incentive.
Staff joining referral bonus Staff who refer a new employee who joins receive a
bonus.

11. Incentive Decisions
Approvers: The approver for an incentive can be the Department Head, HR or MD,
chosen when the incentive is recorded (any of them can be set as the approver). The
approver, date and amount are saved with the record.
Extra work time payment: Paid per hour. Hours come from the RFID punch beyond the
scheduled time or from an approved extra-duty request.
Eligibility: Incentives apply to every staff member.
Rates: The per-hour rate and the referral amounts are settings defined by HR with
Finance. They can differ by staff group if needed, and are not fixed in the system
design.
