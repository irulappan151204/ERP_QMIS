1. Purpose and Scope
Non-Academics supports the school's operations: stores and inventory, transport,
housekeeping, maintenance, IT, records, cameras, and security. It receives requests from
Academics and every other department and runs its own duty allotments, checklists and
purchase workflows.

3. Transport (Manager, Driver, Conductor)
4. Housekeeping
5. Maintenance

9. Security (gate) [Proposed]

4. Module Details and Workflows
4.1 Dashboard and Analytics
Role-based dashboards with charts; click any chart for detailed data.
Store: stock levels, low-stock alerts, pending requests. Transport: trips today, vehicles
in service, pending maintenance. Housekeeping/Maintenance: open tickets, checklist
completion. JD: all departments, comparisons, budget use. MD: full view with audit
data.
4.2 Inventory Management
Product master: name, category, unit, opening stock, minimum stock level.
Stock in (purchase/new product), stock out (issues), balance, availability view for all
users.
Every product and count is listed and visible to the requesting user.
Items not in the list can be requested as a new product.
Reports: stock register, issue register, low stock, consumption by department.

4.3 Purchase Workflow
Flow:
1 . Store (or any department via store request) raises a purchase requirement
2. Store Officer compares old vendor details (previous prices, quotes, delivery history)
3. JD approves the selected vendor and quantity
4. Checked against the budget defined by Finance (monthly / yearly / event)
5. Purchase order raised → goods received → stock updated
6. Payment handled by Finance (voucher)
New products and library book purchases use dropdown-fetched data so details are not
re-entered.
4.4 Request and Approval (Store Issues)
Flow:
1 . Requester selects the department (e.g., Store) and views available products
2. Chooses items and quantity and selects approver
3. Approvals by higher authority (DA / Principal for Academics; Department Head / JD for
others)
4. Store approves and issues
5. Requester collects; stock reduces automatically
6. If the item is unavailable → becomes a new product / purchase requirement
4.5 Ticketing
Any user raises a ticket to a department (IT repair, cleaning, maintenance, etc.) and
selects the responsible person.
Status: Raised → Assigned → In progress → Resolved → Confirmed by requester.
Not resolved or not accepted by the requester → escalates to the next higher
authority (same logic as complaints).
Full history retained; no edit or delete.
4.6 Duty Allotment
Department Head assigns duties by staff, shift, area and date.
Staff see their own duties; heads see the department roster.
Applies to Housekeeping, Maintenance, Transport, Security, Camera Room and others.
Linked to attendance and leave: if a staff member is absent, the head reassigns and the
change is logged.

4.7 Checklists
Types: weekly, monthly, yearly (daily [Proposed]).
Created by the Department Head, completed by staff, verified by the head, reviewed
by JD.
Items marked done with date, time, person and optional photo.
Overdue checklists notify the head and escalate to JD.
Examples: classroom cleaning, washroom checks, fire-safety equipment, vehicle
fitness, electrical inspections.
4.8 Transport Management
Vehicle details, routes, stops, driver and conductor allocation.
Transport Manager saves vehicle data, duty allotment and vehicle records.
Student transport mapping: which student uses which bus; own vs school transport
shown in the student profile.
TC NOC: the Transport Manager approves in the TC workflow if the student uses
school transport.
4.9 Vehicle Records
Registration, insurance, FC, permit, pollution and expiry dates with alerts.
Service history, repairs, fuel and mileage logs, accident or incident records.
Documents uploaded and linked to each vehicle.
4.10 Daily Trip Updates (Driver / Conductor)
Driver and conductor update onboard and offboard status for each student, for both
home → school and school → home trips.
Trip record includes driver and conductor details, route, time and vehicle.
Parents see trip status through the student profile; Transport Manager and Academics
(class teacher) can view.
4.11 Announcements and Instructions
Inter-department announcements and instructions from JD / Department Heads with
target audience selection.
Notifications to selected departments or roles.
4.12 Lost and Found
Anyone who finds an item takes a photo and posts it in announcements.

7. Integrations
8. Confirmed Decisions
Updates from review. These supersede earlier wording in sections 1 to 7 where they differ.
1 . Purchase and approval limits: Not hard-coded. Finance sets the approval limits per
role (for Academics and Non-Academics alike). A Non-Academic Department Head can
approve up to the limit set by Finance; above it, the request is redirected to Finance
by the head. No fixed amount thresholds are defined in the ERP design.
2. Checklists: Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly checklists are all supported.
3. Transport and GPS: Every bus has GPS. The ERP links to the GPS feed so that
authorised users (Transport Manager, class teacher chain, and the child's profile view)
can see the route and live bus location.

4. Driver screen: Drivers and conductors have a separate screen only for onboarding
and offboarding, by trip type: pick up from home or pick up from school. One bus can
run multiple scheduled trips or routes at different times. Before the first trip, the
Transport Manager configures the start time, end time, route map and the student list.
5. Transport data from Finance: Student details and fee payment status (paid or
pending) sync directly from Finance into the transport records.
6. Camera Room: A separate department. When any issue is identified, the Camera
Room marks and stores the relevant footage. Any Department Head can send a
footage request to the Camera Room.
7. File Room: Handles file maintenance and record keeping. Access and issue requests
are approved by the requester's higher department authority.
8. Security: Documented as a department with two roles (see 9.1).
9. PRO: Responsible for government liaison (see 9.2).
1 0. Housekeeping and Maintenance: Kept as separate departments with separate heads
and staff.
1 1 . Store, returnable items: When a product is issued from the store, the issuer marks it
Returnable or Non-Returnable with details. Returnable items are tracked until
returned.
1 2. Store, product out: Any store-related product leaving the premises needs
confirmation from the owning department.
1 3. Store, new product in: When a new product arrives, it automatically goes to the
owning department for confirmation before it is added to stock.
