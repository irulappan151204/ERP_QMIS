1. Purpose and Scope
The MD is the top authority. The MD module gives complete visibility of every
department, the final approval on key processes, and the audit trail of how the system is
used. The MD page is filled with charts, diagrams and comparisons, and every chart
opens into the detailed data behind it.
The MD can view everything but cannot edit or delete any record, the same global rule
as all users.
2. Role and Reporting Line
MD is at the top of every hierarchy:
Academics: DA → Principal → Coordinator → Teacher
Non-Academics: Joint Director → Joint Director Assistant → Department Heads
HR, Finance, Audit: respective heads report to the MD
Audit is Informed to the MD in the RASCI model

8. Confirmed Decisions
Updates from review. These supersede earlier wording in sections 1 to 7 where they differ.
1 . MD role: There is one MD role only. No Chairman or Deputy MD role is created.
2. Approvals: No approval items beyond those in 4.7 are needed. The MD views all data
across every department in addition to the approvals. The vacancy and offer approval
marked [Proposed] in 4.7 is optional and stays off unless you choose to switch it on.
3. Message access logging: Yes. Every time the MD opens a user's messages, the access
is logged in the audit trail (who, whose messages, when).
4. Daily summary: Yes. The MD receives a daily summary notification/email at a set time
(default 8:00 AM, adjustable in settings) with key numbers, pending approvals,
escalations and audit alerts.
5. Devices: The MD uses both phone and desktop. The dashboard must work as a
responsive web app (and mobile app if built) with the same data on both. Approvals
can be given from the phone.
6. Reports to the MD: All the important work reports reach the MD automatically.
